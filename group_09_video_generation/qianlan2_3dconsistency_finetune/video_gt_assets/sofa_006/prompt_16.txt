In a 360-degree view against a plain, pure-color background, this three-seater sofa showcases a sophisticated, modern design. The sofa is upholstered in a rich, dark charcoal fabric with a subtle texture that adds depth. The backrest features plush, removable cushions that invite relaxation, while the armrests are wide and slightly sloped, providing comfort and support. The seat cushions are thick and generously padded, ensuring a cozy seating experience. The overall silhouette is sleek and rectangular, complemented by a low profile that enhances its contemporary appeal.
