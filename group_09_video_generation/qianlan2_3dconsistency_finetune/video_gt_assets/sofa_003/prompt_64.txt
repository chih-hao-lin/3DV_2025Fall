This 360-degree video presents a sleek, modern sofa, with its seamless, flowing design. The sofa features a single-piece structure with a low, curved profile, gracefully transitioning from backrest to seat. Upholstered in a sophisticated deep olive green fabric, its surface is subtly pleated, enhancing a textured appearance. The plush cushioning provides depth and comfort, marked by gentle tufting at the center of the seat and back. As the view rotates, the minimalist design is evident from every angle, with soft, rounded corners and smooth, continuous lines. Set against a plain background, the sofa’s elegant simplicity stands out, adding a touch of contemporary flair to any space.
