This 360-degree video showcases a traditional conical Asian hat, intricately designed with a vibrant yellow and orange checkered pattern. The hat features artistic calligraphy in black, adding a cultural touch. It also displays detailed images of a lion, symbolizing strength and protection. The surface has a subtle texture, highlighting craftsmanship. As the hat rotates, its shape, a perfect cone, becomes apparent. The hat's top is finished with a small, smooth brown tip, complementing its overall aesthetic. Set against a plain, pure-color background, the hat's vivid details stand out, emphasizing both tradition and artistry.
