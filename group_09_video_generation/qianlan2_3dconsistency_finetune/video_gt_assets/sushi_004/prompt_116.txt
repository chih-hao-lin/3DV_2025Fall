This 3D object represents an eclectic collection of sushi pieces, artfully arranged on a checkered grid. Each piece varies in size, with cylindrical forms wrapped in a dark seaweed exterior encasing a rich medley of fillings. The textures range from the glossy, smooth surface of a salmon-topped roll to the matte finish of another with a creamy topping. Subtle details like the glistening rice and vibrant hues of orange and pale yellow fillings create a visually enticing composition. The entire display rotates smoothly, allowing every angle to reveal intricate details, seen against a minimalist, plain-colored background.
