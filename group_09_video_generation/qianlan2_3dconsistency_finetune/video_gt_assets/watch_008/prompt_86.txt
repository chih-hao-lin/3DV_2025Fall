The 3D watch features a playful monkey design as its central theme. The watch face is integrated into the monkey's face, which is round with cute, expressive features. The case is crafted from brown material, making the monkey appear lively and friendly. The body of the monkey extends downward slightly, adding dimension. Its embrace-like arms wrap around the strap of the watch, creating a cohesive look. The strap is a contrasting gray, smooth and flexible, designed for comfort. The watch sits on a plain, pure-color background, emphasizing its whimsical charm in the 360-degree view.
