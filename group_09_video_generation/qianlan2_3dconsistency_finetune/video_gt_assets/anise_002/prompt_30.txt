In this 360-degree video, the intricate 3D model of a star anise is displayed against a plain, pure-color background. The object showcases its distinct star-shaped structure, composed of eight woody, dark brown pods. Each pod is elongated, with a glossy, slightly curved appearance, ending in sharp tips. Inside, small, smooth seeds are visible, adding to the textural contrast. The surface of the pods exhibits subtle ridges and natural imperfections, highlighting the organic character of the star anise. The lighting accentuates the depth and shadows, bringing out its warm, earthy tones and creating a captivating visual experience.
