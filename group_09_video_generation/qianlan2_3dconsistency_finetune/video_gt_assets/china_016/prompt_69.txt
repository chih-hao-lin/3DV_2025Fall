The object is a delicate 3D figurine in the form of a mystical creature, featuring a smooth, glossy surface with a predominantly white body accented by vibrant blue designs. Its sculptural form is both abstract and organic, with flowing curves resembling waves or clouds. The central structure rises to a graceful peak, while each end flares out in a series of symmetrical, fin-like extensions. The intricate blue detailing winds elegantly across the surfaces, adding depth and a sense of motion. Set against a plain background, this piece captivates with its unique blend of simplicity and ornamentation, showcased through a continuous 360-degree view.
