This 360-degree view video showcases a detailed 3D model of a Stegosaurus. The dinosaur features a vibrant orange color with textured skin, capturing the ruggedness of ancient reptiles. Along its back are large, alternating bony plates, adding a distinct prehistoric touch. Its small head, positioned low, is accompanied by a powerful tail adorned with spikes. Set against a plain, pure-color background, the focus remains entirely on the Stegosaurus, offering an immersive and close-up examination of its intricate design and lifelike characteristics.
