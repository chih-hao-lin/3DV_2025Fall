This is a 3D model of a Stegosaurus, renowned for its distinctive features and vibrant coloration. Its body exhibits a warm orange hue, with a gradient that slightly deepens along its spine. The most striking feature is the double row of tall, bony plates that jut skyward along its back, each plate smoothly transitioning from the orange of the body to a pale, cream color at the tips. The creature's stout legs are built for support, and its tail ends in a quartet of menacing spikes, angled backward, emphasizing its defensive capabilities. The entire model rotates gracefully against a plain backdrop, allowing for a full 360-degree view that showcases the intricate details of this prehistoric marvel.
