This captivating 3D model presents a vibrant Stegosaurus, vividly shaded in orange and adorned with distinctive, alternating plates along its back. Each plate showcases a darker hue, creating a striking contrast. The creature's robust body and sturdy legs are highlighted, emphasizing its prehistoric majesty. Its tail tapers elegantly, ending in pointed spikes, serving both a defensive and aesthetic purpose. The Stegosaurus’ textured skin mimics realistic scales, adding depth and dimension to its appearance. Set against a plain background, the model rotates to offer a comprehensive 360-degree view, illuminating every intricate detail of this ancient marvel.
