The 3D object is a uniquely designed ride-on toy resembling a skateboard with a seat. Its body is a sleek, aerodynamic shape made of smooth plastic in a rich rust color. This imaginative design is set against a plain, pure-colored background, enhancing its dynamic appearance as it rotates in a 360-degree view.
