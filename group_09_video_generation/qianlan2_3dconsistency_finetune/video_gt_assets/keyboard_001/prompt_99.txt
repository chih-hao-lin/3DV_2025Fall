This 3D video showcases a retro-style keyboard, viewed in 360 degrees against a plain background. The keyboard features a beige casing with distinctive elements: two rows of maroon keys, a singular row of cream buttons at the top, and a set of arrow keys plus a numeric pad on the right. The sides reveal a slightly angled, ergonomic design. As it rotates, the back is visible, displaying small anti-slip pads and a neatly routed cable channel. The keyboard combines vintage aesthetics with functionality, all highlighted in its unique design and earthy color palette.
