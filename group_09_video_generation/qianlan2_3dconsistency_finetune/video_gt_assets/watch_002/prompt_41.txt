This 360-degree video showcases a unique, abstract 3D object resembling a wristwatch. The object features a broad, rectangular face in a muted gray tone, offering an industrial aesthetic. The timepiece appears suspended between two extended, textured blue bands with a distinct pixelated pattern, suggesting the idea of straps. These bands taper off slightly, adding to the intriguing shape. Illuminated under soft lighting, the object rotates smoothly, revealing the blend of sharp angles and rounded edges. Set against a simple black background, the piece stands out as a contemporary, artistic interpretation of a traditional timepiece.
