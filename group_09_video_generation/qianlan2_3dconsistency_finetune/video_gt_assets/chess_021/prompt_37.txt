This is a 3D model of a classic bishop chess piece. The piece is crafted in a light wood color with a smooth finish, conveying a polished elegance. Its distinct features include a rounded top, slightly tapering inwards, crowned by a small sphere, giving it a refined touch. Below, the piece displays a series of gently curved ridges, adding to its sculptural quality. On one side, characteristic of a bishop, is a subtle diagonal cut, enhancing its distinctive silhouette. The video provides a seamless 360-degree view against a plain backdrop, allowing every detail and curve to be admired fully.
