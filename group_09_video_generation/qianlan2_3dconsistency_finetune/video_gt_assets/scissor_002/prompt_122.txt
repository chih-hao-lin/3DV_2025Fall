This video presents a 360-degree view of a unique, stylized pair of scissors against a plain, pure-color background. The scissors feature irregular, textured blades with a stone-like appearance, diverging from the typical sleek metal design. The handles are rounded loops, seemingly crafted from a dark, matte material, providing a stark contrast to the rugged blades. As the object rotates, the intricate detailing and rough texture of the blades become more apparent, lending the scissors an artisanal, sculptural quality. The seamless rotation offers a comprehensive look at both the functionality and artistic design of the object.
