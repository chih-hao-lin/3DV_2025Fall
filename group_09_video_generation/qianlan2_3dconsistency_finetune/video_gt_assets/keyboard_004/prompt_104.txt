This video showcases a sleek 3D model of a classic keyboard against a plain background. The keyboard features a retro beige color, with a full set of chiclet-style keys, carefully spaced on the textured surface. The keys are slightly raised, offering a tactile feel. The keyboard's ergonomic shape and integrated wrist rest design emphasize comfort. As the camera rotates 360 degrees, every angle reveals its balanced and efficient design, exemplifying a blend of functionality and minimalist elegance.
