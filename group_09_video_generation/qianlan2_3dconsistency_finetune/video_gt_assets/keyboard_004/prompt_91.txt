This 3D object is a classic QWERTY keyboard with a sleek, modern design. It features a matte black finish with a compact layout, including a numeric keypad. The keys are ergonomically spaced and slightly raised, providing a comfortable typing experience. Above the function keys, there are small indicator lights for caps lock, number lock, and scroll lock. The keyboard has a slight incline, contributing to user comfort. On the back, there are adjustable tilt legs and rubber grips to prevent sliding. It's set against a plain, pure-color background, allowing the details to stand out as it undergoes a 360-degree rotation, showcasing its robust yet elegant construction.
