This video showcases a 3D model of a traditional hammer, featuring a robust wooden handle and a metallic head. The head displays a prominent claw design at one end, perfect for nail removal, while the opposite end is flat for driving nails. The textured handle provides a comfortable grip, enhancing control during use. The metallic surface of the head subtly reflects light, highlighting its well-worn, utilitarian look. Set against a plain, monochromatic background, the hammer is presented in a seamless 360-degree rotation, allowing for detailed examination of its practical and classic design from all angles.
