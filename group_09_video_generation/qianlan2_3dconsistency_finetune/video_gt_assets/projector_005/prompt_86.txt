This 3D projector features a sleek, matte black finish with a subtly curved top. Its front panel showcases a perforated design, resembling a speaker grille, giving it a modern appearance. The lens is positioned centrally, enclosed within a glossy frame that adds a touch of sophistication. Along the sides and back, ventilation slots are methodically arranged to ensure optimal cooling. Despite its compact structure, multiple input ports are carefully integrated into the rear, allowing easy connectivity. The overall aesthetic is minimalistic and contemporary, set against a plain, pure-color background, enhancing its technological allure in a 360-degree view.
