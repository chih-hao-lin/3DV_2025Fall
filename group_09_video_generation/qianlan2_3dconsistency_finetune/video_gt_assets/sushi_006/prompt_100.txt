This 3D sushi platter features an elegant arrangement of nigiri and gunkan sushi. The nigiri showcases vibrant orange salmon, expertly placed atop a bed of vinegared rice, while the gunkan sports a dainty seaweed wrap filled with popping yellow roe that glistens. Other pieces include mounds of seasoned rice topped with succulent shrimp and various seafood delights. Each piece is intricately crafted, capturing the delicate textures and colors, from the pearly white rice to the subtly marbled fish. The entire presentation sits on a sleek, checkered board, enhancing the minimalist, refined aesthetic.
