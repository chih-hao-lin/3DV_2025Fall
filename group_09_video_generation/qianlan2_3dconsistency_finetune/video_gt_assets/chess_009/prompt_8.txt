This 3D video features a traditional Chinese chess piece known as Xiangqi. The piece is crafted from light-colored wood, showcasing natural grain patterns that give it a classic appearance. On the top surface, intricate red Chinese characters are elegantly engraved, adding cultural significance and contrast to the smooth, matte finish wooden body. The piece is disc-shaped, and as the camera rotates 360 degrees, each angle reveals the meticulous craftsmanship and balanced proportions of the design. The plain, pure-color backdrop accentuates its artistic detail and cultural essence.
