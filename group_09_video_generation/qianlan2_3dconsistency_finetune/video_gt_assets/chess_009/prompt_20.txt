This video showcases a detailed 3D view of a traditional chess piece from Xiangqi, the Chinese version of chess. The object is a cylindrical, light wooden piece with a smooth, polished finish that highlights the natural grain patterns across its circular surface. On one face, vibrant, embossed red Chinese characters contrast the soft wood, adding cultural significance and aesthetic charm. The characters are encircled by subtly carved concentric rings, enhancing the piece's intricate design. The reverse side is unadorned, revealing more of the wood's organic texture. A pure-color background provides a simple, elegant contrast in this 360-degree presentation.
