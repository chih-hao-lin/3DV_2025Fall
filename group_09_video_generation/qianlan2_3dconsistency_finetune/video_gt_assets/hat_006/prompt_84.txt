This 360-degree video showcases a 3D bucket hat in a camouflage pattern. The hat features a wide brim and a slightly tapered crown, designed for casual outdoor wear. The intricate camo design blends shades of green, black, and brown, offering a rugged, stylish appearance. Textured fabric adds depth to the pattern, while subtle stitching details enhance its sturdiness. As the camera smoothly revolves around the hat, the patterns shift subtly, demonstrating both the texture and the versatility of the design against a plain background.
