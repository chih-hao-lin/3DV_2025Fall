This 360-degree video showcases a 3D model of a star anise, set against a plain, pure-colored background. The object exhibits a natural, earthy brown hue with a distinct, star-shaped pattern typical of dried anise. Its multiple pointed petals, which diverge from a central core, create a captivating and intricate form. The petals are textured, displaying subtle curves and ridges that add to the object's complexity. As the view rotates, the symmetry and artistic elegance of the star anise are highlighted, revealing details of the seed pods nestled within the petal structures.
