In this 360-degree video, an intricately designed 3D model of an acoustic guitar takes center stage. The guitar features a rich wooden texture with natural grain patterns adding depth and authenticity. Its body is a classic hourglass shape, tapering elegantly at the neck. The warm tones of the wood contrast beautifully with the darker, polished fretboard. The bridge and tuning pegs glisten subtly, reflecting light as the camera moves. The headstock has a traditional design with visible tuning machines. Its craftsmanship is evident in the delicate curves and detailed finishes, presented against a plain, pure-color background.
