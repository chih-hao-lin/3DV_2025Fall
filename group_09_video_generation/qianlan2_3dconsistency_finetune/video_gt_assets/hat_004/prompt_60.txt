This video showcases a detailed 3D model of a traditional conical Asian hat crafted from woven straw. Viewed against a plain background, the 360-degree rotation highlights the intricate weave patterns creating a symmetrical, textured surface. The subtle color variation between the golden hues adds depth to the design. The apex of the cone is neatly pointed, while the base is flawlessly circular, emphasizing its structural balance. A distinctive black pattern resembling a leaf is elegantly positioned on the hat's surface, offering a unique artistic touch to the otherwise natural aesthetic.
