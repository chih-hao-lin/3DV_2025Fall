This video presents a 360-degree view of a traditional conical straw hat set against a plain background. The hat showcases a meticulously woven texture, reflecting a warm, natural golden hue. Its structure elegantly tapers to a pointed peak, with darker strands woven into the design to form an intricate pattern, resembling leaves or ferns. This artistic detail adds a touch of uniqueness to the hat's rustic appearance. As the hat rotates, you can appreciate the uniformity and craftsmanship of the weave, highlighting its cultural significance and artisanal quality.
