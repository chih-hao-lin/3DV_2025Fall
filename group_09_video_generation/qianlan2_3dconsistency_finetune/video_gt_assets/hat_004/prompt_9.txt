This video presents a detailed 360-degree view of a traditional conical hat, commonly known as a "rice hat" or "Asian straw hat." Crafted from woven straw, the hat features a distinctive pointed top that gradually widens to form a broad circular brim, offering substantial sun protection. The straw's natural, earthy tones create a textured appearance, and the surface bears circular patterning from the weaving technique. Subtle dark green accents, resembling leaf patterns, embellish parts of the hat, adding a touch of nature-inspired decoration. The plain, pure-color background ensures that the hat remains the focal point of the video.
