This 360-degree video showcases a traditional Asian conical hat, known as a "rice hat" or "coolie hat," set against a plain background. The hat is intricately woven from natural straw, forming a symmetrical, broad-brimmed cone that tapers to a point at the top. Its surface reveals a beautiful, checkerboard pattern created by the interlacing of the straw fibers, with sporadic decorative elements giving it a unique flair. The hat’s earthy, golden hue reflects light subtly, highlighting its craftsmanship and cultural authenticity. The hat spins slowly, allowing viewers to appreciate its simple yet elegant design from every angle, offering a glimpse into the rich cultural heritage it represents.
