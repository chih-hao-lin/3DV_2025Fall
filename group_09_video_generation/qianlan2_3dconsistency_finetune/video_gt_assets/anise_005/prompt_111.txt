This 360-degree video captures a detailed 3D model of a star anise on a plain, solid background. The star anise displays its iconic star-like shape, with eight woody, pointed pods radiating symmetrically from the center. Each pod is robust and textured, showcasing a rich blend of earthy browns and muted grays, with a slightly glossy surface. The central hub is compact, with intricate grooves adding dimension. As the view circulates, the individual folds and curves of the pods become more pronounced, illustrating the complexity and natural beauty of the star anise's structure.
