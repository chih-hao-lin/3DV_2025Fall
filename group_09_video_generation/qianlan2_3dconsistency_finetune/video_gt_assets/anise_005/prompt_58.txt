This 3D animation presents a detailed star anise, showcasing its iconic star-shaped form. With eight pointy, petal-like arms, the dark brown texture is rough and slightly woody, evoking a natural, earthy feel. The central core connects each arm seamlessly, creating a harmonious radial symmetry. As the view rotates, the subtle grooves and ridges are accentuated by the lighting, highlighting the intricate details. The pure-colored background remains unobtrusive, ensuring full focus on the anise's elegant and complex design.
