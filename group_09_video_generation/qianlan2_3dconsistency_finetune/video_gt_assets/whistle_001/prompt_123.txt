The object is a compact, abstract piece resembling a whistle, with a glossy finish. Its design features a bright yellow, bell-shaped base that tapers slightly upward. Two rounded, brown sections encircle the midsection, adding visual contrast and dimension. A smoothly curved handle arches gracefully from the top to the side, also in a rich brown shade, creating an elegant and ergonomic appearance. The top is rounded, completing the form. The entire piece is set against a plain, pure-color background, allowing its vibrant, sculptural qualities to stand out during the 360-degree view.
