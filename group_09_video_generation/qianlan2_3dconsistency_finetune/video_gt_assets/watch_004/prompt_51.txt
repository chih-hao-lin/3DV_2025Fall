This 3D-printed smartwatch prototype features a square face with rounded edges, encapsulated in a soft pink casing. It displays a distinct, unfinished texture, indicating an experimental design phase. The plain, muted strap, in an off-white hue, arcs gently around the wrist, maintaining a cohesive connection to the watch face, which is marked by subtle, minimalistic button details on its side. The plain-colored background emphasizes the watch, highlighting its experimental nature and unique charm as the watch gracefully rotates in a 360-degree view.
