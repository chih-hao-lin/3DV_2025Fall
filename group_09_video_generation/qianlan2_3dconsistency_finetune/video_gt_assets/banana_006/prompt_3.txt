This video presents a 3D model of an entire banana, showcasing a 360-degree view against a plain background. The banana is realistically rendered, featuring its characteristic elongated, curvilinear form and vibrant yellow skin. Subtle brown speckles adorn its surface, adding to its lifelike appearance. The tips of the banana are slightly darker, depicting the natural ripening process. As the camera circles the object, the smooth, slightly glossy texture of the peel is highlighted, offering a comprehensive view from every angle and emphasizing its fresh, tropical essence.
