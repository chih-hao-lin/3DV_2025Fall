In this 360-degree video, an intricately detailed 3D star anise pod unfolds against a plain backdrop. Each of its eight brown, boat-shaped carpels curves gracefully, resembling delicate petals that meet at the center. The surface texture is rugged, displaying natural creases and subtle luster that reflect light softly as the pod rotates. The rich, earthy tones of the pod range from warm browns to deeper hues, capturing the organic complexity of this aromatic spice. Its hollow interior offers glimpses of the glossy seeds within, adding to the intricacy of this botanical sculpture.
