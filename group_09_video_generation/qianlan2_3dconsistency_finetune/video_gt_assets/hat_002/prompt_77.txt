This 360-degree view showcases a vintage wide-brimmed hat crafted from woven straw. The hat features subtle alternations of light and dark green stripes that encircle both the brim and the crown. The crown's pattern presents a delicate, zigzag chevron weave, adding a touch of elegance. Around the base of the crown, a rustic braided rope is tied into a decorative knot, complete with a charming bow, enhancing the hat’s classic aesthetic. The brim is slightly frayed at the edges, giving it a weathered, antique look. Set against a plain background, the video emphasizes the intricate texture and design of this timeless accessory.
