This video showcases a 3D model of a desiccated, star-shaped anise pod in remarkable detail. The pod features eight distinct, elongated arms, each slightly curved and hardened. The outer surface is rugged and textured, displaying varying shades of warm brown. As the camera completes a 360-degree loop, the intricate structure of the pod is accentuated, revealing its hollow interior and rustic, woody appearance. Each arm holds a small, glossy seed, visible through the pod's open design, making it a captivating visual on the minimalist background.
